The person behind the projects

My story, in my own words.

I am Okasha Awan, a 17-year-old entrepreneur from Karachi, Pakistan. My journey is still in progress. It began with learning graphic design and the decision to build something independently. I am sharing the real steps—not claiming that the journey is finished.

Learning before launching

Before starting my first business, I completed a graphic design course to learn the skills I needed. I wanted to turn that learning into practical work and build something of my own.

Building independently

I began alone, without a team. I handled the founder, manager, designer and day-to-day worker responsibilities myself. Every project became an opportunity to learn and improve.

Milestones

The journey so far.

Awan Design Studio — the first step

I started my first online design business, Awan Design Studio. Getting work was difficult at first and there were periods without projects. Gradually, online work began to come in. I kept working, learning and improving—handling the business on my own.

03

Sultan Bahadur Design Studio

As the business developed, I changed the brand name to Sultan Bahadur Design Studio, continuing the creative journey that began with Awan Design Studio.

04

I started a new technology venture and launched its website, with a focus on digital tools for business management.
